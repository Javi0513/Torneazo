Delete your account and all your data

  1. Open TORNEAZO and log in.
  2. Tap your name, at the top right, to open «Your account».
  3. Tap «Delete my account and my data».
  4. Type BORRAR and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account (email and password) and all your tournaments. If you pay for Premium, cancel the subscription first in «Your account» › «Manage subscription»: deleting the account does not cancel it.

If you can't log in

Write to javiadsense@gmail.com from your account's email, saying what you want to delete.
