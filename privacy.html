Last updated: 15 November 2026. · Español

This is a translation of the Spanish privacy policy. If there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

Who is responsible

Francisco Javier Casado García (Spanish tax ID 15471491N), based in Granada, Spain. Contact: javiadsense@gmail.com.

What data we process

  • Your account: email, password (stored encrypted, we never see it), nickname and, if you upload one, a profile photo.
  • Your tournaments: name, game or sport, participants' names, groups, matches, results, prizes, news, sponsors and any information you write. Without an account, your tournaments are only stored on your device.
  • Sign-ups: when someone signs up from a tournament link, their name and, if the organizer asks for them, a contact and a level.
  • Results sent by players: the name of the sender and the score.
  • Security: to prevent abuse in sign-ups, results and reports we store an encrypted, irreversible fingerprint of the connection address (not the address itself).
  • Phone alerts: if you turn them on from a tournament page, we store your browser's alert address and the player you chose, only to tell you about your matches. You can turn them off from the same page or in your browser settings; they are deleted when the tournament ends or is deleted.
  • QR check-in: if the organizer turns it on and you confirm you have arrived, we store the name you choose from the tournament list and an encrypted fingerprint of the connection.
  • Referees: if someone enters results with a referee link, we store the name they type and the result.
  • Organizer alerts: if you turn on alerts as an organizer, we store your browser's alert address linked to your account, to notify you of sign-ups, results and check-ins. You can turn them off in the app.
  • Discord (optional): if the organizer connects their tournament's Discord channel, we store that channel's link only on the server and post the draw, results and standings there (including participants' names).
  • Payments: the Stripe customer and subscription IDs and the status of your plan.

Why we use it and on what basis

  • To provide the service: logging in, saving and restoring your tournaments, publishing them if you want, and managing Premium (performance of the contract).
  • To protect the app against spam and abuse (legitimate interest).
  • We do not sell your data, we do not use it to profile you and there is no third-party advertising with tracking.

What is public

Only if you create a tournament's public link, anyone with that link will see its name, participants, results, prizes, news and sponsors. If you also show it in the directory, it can be found in «Public tournaments». You can stop sharing it at any time. As the organizer, you are responsible for having permission to publish the participants' names.

Who else has access

  • Supabase (accounts and database, servers in the EU).
  • Stripe (payments; we never see or store your card details).
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting).
  • Google Fonts (fonts): when they load, your browser connects to Google.
  • Have I Been Pwned: when you create or change your password we check whether it appears in known leaks by sending only the first 5 characters of an encrypted fingerprint; your password never leaves your device.
  • Discord: only if the organizer connects their channel; it receives the tournament messages.
  • Twitch: if the organizer adds official streamers, we ask Twitch whether those channels are live. We only send the public channel names.

Each of them processes data only to provide their service to us. Discord, Twitch, Stripe, GitHub and Google may process data outside the EU, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

Ads

The free version shows a JAVI AI house ad. It uses no cookies or tracking: it is a plain link that opens WhatsApp if you tap it. Premium has no ads.

Storage on your device

The app stores your tournament, your preferences (including your language) and a copy to work offline in your browser. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Your rights

You can access your data, download it and delete your account with all its content from «My account» in the app, or by writing to the contact email. You can also ask us to correct or restrict the use of your data, or object to it. If you signed up for someone else's tournament, you can ask the organizer or us to delete your sign-up. If you think we are not handling your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to the authority in your country.

Retention and security

We keep your data while you have an account; when you delete it, it is erased immediately together with your tournaments, sign-ups and results. Each user can only access their own data and the connection is always encrypted.

Minimum age

You must be at least 14 years old to create an account.
